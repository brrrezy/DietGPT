Pro Tips for Success

Meal Timing

Space meals 3-4 hours apart. Eat within 30 minutes post-workout for optimal recovery.

Hydration

Drink water throughout the day. Start with 500ml upon waking to kickstart metabolism.

Portion Control

Use your palm for protein, fist for veggies, cupped hand for carbs, thumb for fats.

Recovery

Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep. Quality rest is crucial for muscle recovery and fat loss.
